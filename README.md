# 🐉 Game of Thrones (TV Series)

**Game of Thrones** is an American fantasy drama television series created by **David Benioff** and **D. B. Weiss** for **HBO**.  
It is based on the *"A Song of Ice and Fire"* novels by **George R. R. Martin**.

---

## 📺 Overview

- **Title:** Game of Thrones  
- **Genre:** Fantasy, Drama, Adventure  
- **Created by:** David Benioff, D. B. Weiss  
- **Based on:** *A Song of Ice and Fire* by George R. R. Martin  
- **Country of Origin:** United States  
- **Original Network:** HBO  
- **Original Run:** April 17, 2011 – May 19, 2019  
- **Number of Seasons:** 8  
- **Number of Episodes:** 73  

---

## 🗺️ Setting

The story takes place in the fictional continents of **Westeros** and **Essos**, following several noble families as they fight for control of the **Iron Throne** and the **Seven Kingdoms**.  
It features themes of **power**, **betrayal**, **honor**, **war**, and **magic**.

## ⚔️ Plot Summary

The series depicts the violent dynastic struggles among the realm's noble families for control of the **Iron Throne**.  
Meanwhile, ancient enemies return from the **North**, and the last descendants of a fallen dynasty seek to reclaim their rightful rule from exile.

---

## 🧊 Awards & Recognition

- **Emmy Awards:** 59 wins (a record for a drama series)  
- **Golden Globe Awards:** Best Television Series – Drama (2018)  
- **Critical Acclaim:** Praised for complex characters, storytelling, and production quality.  
- **Controversy:** Final season received mixed reviews for pacing and character arcs.

---

## 🧙‍♂️ Fun Facts

- Over **10,000** unique costumes were created for the show.  
- The **Dothraki** and **Valyrian** languages were fully developed by a linguist.  
- Filmed across **10 countries**, including Northern Ireland, Croatia, and Spain.  

---

## 📚 Spin-offs

- **House of the Dragon (2022–present)** – a prequel set about 200 years before the events of Game of Thrones, focusing on **House Targaryen**.
